📍 Uran, Maharashtra 🏭 Oil & Gas 👤 BIM Engineer

ONGC Oil & Gas Facility — Structural Health Assessment

The Challenge

Following an on-site accident at this massive 250-acre oil and gas processing plant, there was a critical need to assess the structural stability of the affected areas and facilitate safe reconstruction. The challenge was accurately capturing the complex, real-world conditions of damaged industrial steel structures, pipe racks, storage tanks, and support trestles.

Revit FARO Point Cloud Scan-to-BIM

My Process & Impact

  • Scan-to-BIM Conversion: Using FARO scan data, I reverse-engineered and modeled complex industrial steel structures in Revit — tanks, pipe supports, trestles, multi-level sheds, and equipment foundations.
  • Dimensional Fidelity: Maintained precise accuracy against the point cloud to create a true "digital twin" for structural assessment.
  • Documentation: Produced structural layout sheets with plans, sections, elevations, and cross-sections directly from the coordinated BIM model.
  • Outcome: Provided the exact geometric baseline required for structural stability reports and the subsequent plant reconstruction.

📍 Riyadh, Saudi Arabia 🎢 Entertainment 👤 BIM Team Lead

EXIT 15 — Theming Support Steel (Water Park)

The Challenge

For the Water Park sector of the massive EXIT 15 entertainment complex, I led the BIM delivery for highly complex structural theming steel. The challenge was interpreting engineering calculations and converting external structural models from Tekla into fully detailed, fabrication-ready Revit models — complete with all connections, base plates, anchor bolts, gusset plates, and welding details — all while managing and mentoring a modeling team.

Revit Steel Tekla → IFC Dynamo Navisworks ACC

My Process & Impact

  • IFC Interoperability: Led the workflow to convert Tekla IFC models into native Revit elements, meticulously modeling all steel connections using Revit Steel.
  • Team Leadership: Mentored the modeling team on advanced Revit Steel features, troubleshot technical issues, and kept production on pace.
  • BIM Automation: Introduced custom Dynamo scripts and plugins to accelerate repetitive steel detailing tasks and reduce manual errors.
  • Fabrication Documentation: Produced LOD 350+ shop drawings with connection details, enlarged annotated plans, and coordinate-based layout sheets for on-site erection.

📍 Bengaluru, India 🏢 Commercial 👤 BIM Team Lead

ITC Green Centre — 15-Story Commercial Tower (LOD 450 Facade)

The Challenge

This 15-story commercial tower demanded extremely high-fidelity modeling of its entire exterior facade system. The challenge was developing LOD 450 facade families — not standard curtain walls, but highly complex parametric assemblies containing hundreds of nested sub-components. This was the most technically demanding family creation I had undertaken at that point in my career.

Revit Family Editor Navisworks LOD 450

My Process & Impact

  • Advanced Parametric Families: Personally engineered LOD 450 facade families with hundreds of nested sub-components, managing complex parametric constraints for fabrication-level accuracy.
  • Team Coordination: Led a dedicated team to deploy these families across the entire 15-story building and generate comprehensive sheet documentation.
  • Deliverable: Produced incredibly accurate, data-rich facade models with detailed shop drawings, seamlessly bridging design intent and manufacturing.
