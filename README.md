## Project 1: ONGC Oil & Gas Facility (250 Acres)
**Location:** Uran, Maharashtra, India  
**Sector:** Industrial / Oil & Gas  
**Role:** BIM Engineer (Project Associate L1)  
**Tools Used:** Autodesk Revit, FARO (Point Cloud Data)  


### 1. The Context & Challenge
The ONGC Uran facility is a massive 250-acre oil and gas plant. Following an on-site accident, there was a critical need to assess the structural stability of the affected areas to facilitate safe reconstruction. The primary challenge was accurately capturing the complex, real-world conditions of the damaged industrial steel structures, pipe racks, and storage tanks so that structural engineers could reliably analyze the plant's health.

### 2. My Process (Scan-to-BIM)
I was responsible for delivering the large-scale point cloud-based steel modeling for the structural health assessment. 
*   **Scan-to-BIM Conversion:** Using as-built FARO scan data, I reverse-engineered and modeled the complex industrial steel structures in Revit. 
*   **Dimensional Fidelity:** It was critical to maintain precise dimensional accuracy against the point cloud data to ensure the structural engineers had a true "digital twin" for their condition assessment.

### 3. The Deliverable & Impact
*   **Accurate As-Built Models:** Delivered highly detailed structural models of the facility's tanks, sheds, and support trestles.
*   **Structural Documentation:** Produced accurate structural layout sheets, plans, sections, and elevations directly from the point cloud-verified model.
*   **Outcome:** These deliverables successfully provided the engineering teams with the exact geometric baseline required for their detailed structural stability reports and the subsequent reconstruction of the plant.

## Project 2: EXIT 15 – Theming Support Steel
**Location:** Al Nahda Entertainment Complex (Water Park) - Riyadh, Saudi Arabia  
**Sector:** Entertainment / Leisure  
**Role:** BIM Team Lead (Steel Support)  
**Tools Used:** Autodesk Revit (Revit Steel), Tekla (IFC), Dynamo, Navisworks Manage  

### 1. The Context & Challenge
For the water park sector of the massive EXIT 15 entertainment complex, I led the BIM delivery for the highly complex structural theming steel. The challenge was interpreting engineering calculations and converting external structural models into fully detailed, fabrication-ready models in Revit, all while managing a team that needed guidance on advanced steel detailing.

### 2. My Process & Leadership
*   **Interoperability & Modeling:** Received engineering calculations and structural models via Tekla. I led the workflow to convert these IFC models into native Revit elements, meticulously modeling all complex steel connections using Revit Steel.
*   **Team Leadership:** I mentored the modeling team, helping them understand complex Revit Steel features, troubleshooting their issues, and resolving technical doubts to keep production moving.
*   **BIM Automation:** To accelerate our workflow and reduce manual errors on repetitive tasks, I introduced specialized plugins and wrote custom Dynamo scripts.

### 3. The Deliverable & Impact
*   **Coordinated Fabrication Models:** Delivered highly accurate LOD 350+ steel support models that integrated perfectly with the complex architectural theming and MEP systems.
*   **Increased Efficiency:** The implementation of Dynamo scripts and my direct mentorship empowered the team to work significantly faster, ensuring we hit our delivery milestones while maintaining strict quality standards.

## Project 5: ITC Green Centre – 15-Story Commercial Tower
**Location:** Bengaluru, India  
**Sector:** Commercial  
**Role:** BIM Team Lead (Facade & Family Creation)  
**Tools Used:** Autodesk Revit (Advanced Family Editor), Navisworks  

### 1. The Context & Challenge
This commercial tower project required extremely high-fidelity modeling of its exterior shell. The primary challenge was the development of LOD 450 facade systems. These were not standard curtain walls; they required the creation of highly complex, parametric facade families containing hundreds of nested sub-components. It was the most technically demanding family creation challenge I had faced up to that point.

### 2. My Process & Leadership
*   **Advanced Parametric Family Creation:** I personally engineered and built the intricate LOD 450 facade families. I managed the complex nesting hierarchies and parametric constraints required to ensure the families flexed correctly and contained all the precise geometric data needed for fabrication.
*   **Team Coordination & Documentation:** While I drove the high-level family architecture, I led a dedicated team of modelers to deploy these families across the entire 15-story model. I directed their efforts in generating the extensive sheet extraction and highly detailed 2D representations required for the facade installation.

### 3. The Deliverable & Impact
*   **LOD 450 Facade Models:** Delivered incredibly accurate, data-rich facade models that met stringent LOD 450 fabrication standards.
*   **Comprehensive Drawing Packages:** Under my leadership, the team successfully produced detailed layout sheets and shop drawings directly from these complex families, flawlessly bridging the gap between design intent and manufacturing.
