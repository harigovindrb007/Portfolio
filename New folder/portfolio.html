Project 01

ONGC Oil & Gas Facility — Structural Health Assessment (250 Acres)

📍 Uran, Maharashtra, India 🏭 Industrial / Oil & Gas 👤 BIM Engineer Revit FARO Point Cloud Scan-to-BIM

The Context & Challenge

The ONGC Uran facility is a massive 250-acre oil and gas processing plant. Following an on-site accident, there was a critical need to assess the structural stability of the affected areas and facilitate safe reconstruction. The primary challenge was accurately capturing the complex, real-world conditions of damaged industrial steel structures, pipe racks, storage tanks, and support trestles so that structural engineers could reliably analyze the plant's health.

My Process — Scan-to-BIM

  • Point Cloud-Based Steel Modeling: Using as-built FARO scan data, I reverse-engineered and modeled complex industrial steel structures in Revit — including storage tanks, pipe supports, trestles, multi-level sheds, and equipment foundations.
  • Dimensional Fidelity: Maintained precise dimensional accuracy against the point cloud data, ensuring the structural engineers received a true "digital twin" for their condition assessment and reconstruction planning.
  • Documentation Production: Produced accurate structural layout sheets with plans, sections, elevations, and cross-sections directly from the coordinated BIM model.

The Deliverable & Impact

  • Accurate As-Built Models of the facility's tanks, sheds, trestles, and structural support frameworks.
  • Comprehensive Drawing Packages including structural layouts, equipment plans, and detailed engineering reports.
  • Outcome: Successfully provided the engineering teams with the exact geometric baseline required for their structural stability reports and the subsequent plant reconstruction.

Project 02

EXIT 15 — Theming Support Steel (Water Park)

📍 Al Nahda Entertainment Complex, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia 🎢 Entertainment / Leisure 👤 BIM Team Lead Revit Steel Tekla → IFC Dynamo Navisworks

The Context & Challenge

For the Water Park sector of the massive EXIT 15 entertainment complex, I led the BIM delivery for highly complex structural theming steel. The challenge was interpreting engineering calculations and converting external structural models from Tekla into fully detailed, fabrication-ready Revit models — complete with all connections, base plates, and bolting details — all while managing and mentoring a modeling team.

My Process & Leadership

  • Interoperability & Modeling: Received engineering calculations and structural models via Tekla. Led the workflow to convert IFC models into native Revit elements, meticulously modeling all complex steel connections using Revit Steel — including base plates, gusset plates, stiffeners, anchor bolts, and fillet welds.
  • Team Leadership: Mentored the modeling team on advanced Revit Steel features, troubleshot their technical issues, and resolved doubts to keep production moving at pace.
  • BIM Automation: Introduced specialized plugins and wrote custom Dynamo scripts to accelerate repetitive steel detailing tasks and reduce manual errors.
  • Fabrication-Level Documentation: Produced LOD 350+ shop drawings with comprehensive connection details, enlarged plans with annotations, and coordinate-based layout sheets for on-site steel erection.

The Deliverable & Impact

  • Coordinated Fabrication Models — Highly accurate steel support models integrating seamlessly with complex architectural theming and MEP systems.
  • Detailed Shop Drawings — Connection plate details (plan, section, 3D), column elevations, base plate schedules, and anchor bolt coordinates.
  • Increased Efficiency — Dynamo automation and direct mentorship empowered the team to deliver significantly faster while maintaining strict quality standards.

Project 03

ITC Green Centre — 15-Story Commercial Tower (LOD 450 Facade)

📍 Bengaluru, India 🏢 Commercial 👤 BIM Team Lead Revit (Advanced Family Editor) Navisworks LOD 450

The Context & Challenge

This 15-story commercial tower project demanded extremely high-fidelity modeling of its entire exterior facade system. The primary challenge was the development of LOD 450 facade families — not standard curtain walls, but highly complex, parametric assemblies containing hundreds of nested sub-components. This was the most technically demanding family creation I had undertaken at that point in my career.

My Process & Leadership

  • Advanced Parametric Family Creation: Personally engineered and built the intricate LOD 450 facade families. Managed complex nesting hierarchies and parametric constraints to ensure families flexed correctly and contained all precise geometric data needed for fabrication.
  • Team Coordination & Documentation: Led a dedicated team of modelers to deploy these families across the entire 15-story building. Directed their efforts in generating extensive sheet production and highly detailed 2D representations required for facade installation.

The Deliverable & Impact

  • LOD 450 Facade Models — Incredibly accurate, data-rich facade models meeting stringent fabrication-level standards.
  • Comprehensive Drawing Packages — Detailed layout sheets and shop drawings produced directly from these complex families, bridging the gap between design intent and manufacturing.
